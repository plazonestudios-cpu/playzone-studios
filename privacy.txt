Privacy Policy

PlayZone-Studios provides this application as a free service, intended for use as is, with potential updates in the future. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services. By accessing or using our application, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect two categories of information through our games and services:

1. Personal Information

Personal information refers to data that may identify an individual user. We only collect limited personal data automatically, such as your IP address and Advertising ID, to improve user experience.

We do not collect additional personal details automatically. Payment information, including credit card details, is never processed or stored by us. All purchases made within our apps are securely handled by platforms such as Google or Apple.

We collect personal data only with your explicit consent, typically when you accept the privacy policy and terms within the application.

2. Non-Personal Information

Non-personal information includes anonymous usage data related to your device and interactions. This may include:

Device identifiers and technical specifications
Game progress, playtime, scores, and achievements
IP address, country, time zone, and session duration
Browser type, language, and network details
Device model, operating system, and carrier information
Advertising identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, Advertising ID)
Other technical and diagnostic data

This information is used solely to enhance our services and improve user experience.

How We Use Your Information

The data we collect is used to operate and improve our services. This may include:

Delivering and evaluating advertisements
Enhancing and developing our games and features
Detecting and fixing bugs or technical issues
